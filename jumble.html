HOW TO
PLAY

  • Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 grid.
  • Fill a full row or column to blast it and score points — chain clears back-to-back for a rising combo multiplier.
  • Clearing the whole board earns a Perfect Clear bonus — dealing quietly favors hands that can finish one off once the board's already close to empty.
  • You're never dealt a hand that's unplayable from the start — but misplaying can still box you in.
  • ↩️ Undo reverts your last placement (if it didn't clear a line). 🔀 Reroll swaps your hand once per game.
  • Settings ⚙️ has Zen Mode (never lose — a stuck hand reshuffles), Hard Mode (no Undo or Reroll), colorblind-friendly shape marks, and 15+ visual themes.
